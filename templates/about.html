About NetMap AI

NetMap AI is a B.Tech CSE project designed to study internet connectivity. It scores the browser measurements it can obtain and maps opted-in, user-owned measurement locations. Browser-based tests do not currently provide Wi-Fi signal strength or packet loss, so those metrics are excluded rather than estimated.

Key Features:

  • Session-based accounts and private measurement history
  • Browser connectivity tests with unavailable metrics left unset
  • Opt-in location mapping with manual location selection
  • Configurable scoring and measurement charts
